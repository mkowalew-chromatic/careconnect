New React Native (Expo) patient portal for iOS and Android: patient sign-in with the session kept in the device keychain, home with the next visit, visits list and detail (paperwork status, check-in, cancel), secure messages (inbox, thread, reply), and the full booking flow from visit details through time, patient information and review to confirmation. Its Storybook (every screen state from fixed fixtures, plus the native design-system stories) is captured by Chromatic on iOS and Android.
